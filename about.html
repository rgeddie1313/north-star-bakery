About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: bring neighbors together over handmade food. Our sample bakery story celebrates the daily routines and special occasions of our community.

Our sourcing philosophy

We value dependable ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Our goal is to work with nearby suppliers when possible and choose seasonal ingredients that suit our baked goods.

Meet the team

Our bread bakers

The baking team shapes the daily loaves and prepares the Signature Loaf for customers to enjoy at home.

Our pastry and cake team

Our pastry makers prepare morning treats and help plan cakes for family celebrations and local events.

Our counter team

Our counter staff welcomes neighbors and helps customers choose products and discuss pre-order requests.
